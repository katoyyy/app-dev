Forrest Gump
“Life is like a box of chocolates. You never know what you're gonna get.”
— Forrest Gump

Forrest Gump is a 1994 American drama film directed by Robert Zemeckis and starring Tom Hanks.
It tells the story of a kind-hearted man with a low IQ who unintentionally influences several historical events while simply trying to find his place in the world.

Main Characters
Forrest Gump – played by Tom Hanks
Jenny Curran – played by Robin Wright
Lieutenant Dan Taylor – played by Gary Sinise
Mrs. Gump – played by Sally Field

Key Themes
Perseverance – Forrest never gives up despite challenges.
Love and Friendship – His deep affection for Jenny and loyalty to his friends.
Destiny and Chance – The movie explores how fate and coincidence shape life.

Soundtrack Highlight
The movie’s soundtrack includes timeless classics like:
Sweet Home Alabama by Lynyrd Skynyrd
Fortunate Son by Creedence Clearwater Revival
Imagine by John Lennon
